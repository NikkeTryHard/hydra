## Context
Implement a clean fast-live Mahjong RL path: train directly from Rust-owned compact trajectories and activate frozen actor weights in memory, without waiting for canonical rollout export, trajectory persistence or runtime-control publication. The user wants approximately 100ms generation-transition overhead, short initial preparation and maximum useful training progress toward a strong Mahjong AI; extra epochs/games solely to inflate utilization are prohibited. Preserve the current eight-generation, 128-complete-games-per-generation, one-epoch comparator, one future generation, exact rules/visibility, categorical sampling law and PPO equations. The pipeline/source identity changes explicitly; do not claim bit-identical trajectories versus the old profile after its RNG identities change.
